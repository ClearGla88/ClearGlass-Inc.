## Google Business Profile

| ID | Verbatim | Source | What it changes for ClearGlass |
|---|---|---|---|
| F1 | "To qualify for a Business Profile, a business must make in-person contact with customers during its stated hours." The ineligible list includes "Brands, organizations, artists, and other online-only businesses." | [Business eligibility and ownership guidelines](https://support.google.com/business/answer/13763036?hl=en) | A profile is allowed only if ClearGlass really meets clients in person during stated hours, for example at their offices. A remote-only advisory is not eligible. In that case it never appears in the map pack, and the geo-grid objective does not apply. `[DECISION REQUIRED]` |
| F2 | "If you're a service-area business, you should hide your business address from customers." | [Guidelines for representing your business on Google](https://support.google.com/business/answer/3038177?hl=en) | Hide the address. Keep a home address off the website too. |
| F3 | "Service-area businesses can't list a "virtual" office unless that office is staffed during business hours." | same | No mail drops and no unstaffed co-working addresses. |
| F4 | "The boundaries of your profile's overall service area shouldn't extend farther than about 2 hours of driving time from where your business is based." | same | Burlington, Halton and Hamilton fit. Leave "Canada" and "United States" out of the profile's service area, even though the website's structured data lists them. |
| F5 | "Your name should reflect your business's real-world name, as used consistently on your storefront, website, stationery, and as known to customers." | same | Use one name, the real one, for example "ClearGlass Inc.". "ClearGlassInc Artemis" and "ClearGlass Burlington Cybersecurity" are not acceptable profile names. |
| F6 | "Local results are mainly based on relevance, distance, and popularity." On prominence: "This factor's also based on info like how many websites link to your business and how many reviews you have." | [Tips to improve your local ranking on Google](https://support.google.com/business/answer/7091?hl=en) | Distance from the business location can't be changed. Grid cells in Oakville, Hamilton, Milton and Dundas may stay red whatever the content does. The controllable levers are relevance (accurate profile and site), reviews and links. |
| F7 | "There's no way to request or pay for a better local ranking on Google." | same | Any vendor that promises paid local-pack placement is wrong or breaking the rules. |
| F8 | Prohibited: "Discourage or prohibit negative reviews, or selectively solicit positive reviews from customers" and "Offer incentives – such as payment, discounts, free goods and/or services - in exchange for posting any review or revision or removal of a negative review." | [Maps user-generated content policy](https://support.google.com/contributionpolicy/answer/7400114?hl=en) | The program prompt suggests review requests "triggered by project completion or NPS score". Using an NPS score to decide who gets asked is selective solicitation. The trigger is completion only, every eligible client gets the same request, and no incentive is offered. |
| F9 | "The My Business Q&A API was discontinued on November 3, 2025. You can no longer read or post questions and answers using the API." | [Q&A API change log](https://developers.google.com/my-business/content/qanda/change-log) | "Q&A monitoring" is removed from every agent and plan. |
| F10 | "You can ask questions and get an AI-powered response, with the ability to go deeper through follow-up questions and helpful links to the web, from Ask Maps." | [Ask questions in Google Maps](https://support.google.com/maps/answer/16842041?hl=en) | Questions about places now go through an AI feature. There is no Q&A section to seed. Accurate profile and website content is what is left to control. |
| F11 | "Manage a Google Business Profile that is verified and active for 60+ days." Then: "Request access to the Business Profile API via the GBP API contact form and await approval." | [Business Profile API prerequisites](https://developers.google.com/my-business/content/prereqs) | API automation can't start in days 1 to 14. Use manual exports until the profile is 60+ days old and access is approved. |

## Google Search

| F12 | Doorway abuse examples include "Having multiple domain names or pages targeted at specific regions or cities that funnel users to one page". | [Spam policies for Google web search](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials/spam-policies) | Build one real Burlington page. ## Canada's Anti-Spam Legislation (S.C. 2010, c. 23)

Source for all rows: [Justice Laws, full text](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/acts/E-1.6/FullText.html).

| ID | Provision | Verbatim | What it changes for ClearGlass |
|---|---|---|---|
| F17 | s. 10(9)(b) | Consent is implied if "the person to whom the message is sent has conspicuously published, or has caused to be conspicuously published, the electronic address to which the message is sent, the publication is not accompanied by a statement that the person does not wish to receive unsolicited commercial electronic messages at the electronic address and the message is relevant to the person's business, role, functions or duties in a business or official capacity" | Partnership emails go only to addresses an organization publishes for that kind of request, only when the page carries no "no solicitations" statement, and only about that role. Record the URL of the page where the address was published. `[LEGAL REVIEW]` |
| F18 | s. 10(10)(a) | An existing business relationship arises from "the purchase or lease of a product, goods, a service, land or an interest or right in land, within the two-year period immediately before the day on which the message was sent" | An emailed review request to a paying client relies on this two-year window from the purchase. |
| F19 | s. 10(10)(e) | "an inquiry or application, within the six-month period immediately before the day on which the message was sent" | Someone who only inquired can be emailed for six months from the inquiry. |
| F20 | s. 11(2) and 11(3) | The unsubscribe address or page must be "valid for a minimum of 60 days after the message has been sent"; requests take effect "without delay, and in any event no later than 10 business days after the indication has been sent" | Every commercial email carries a working unsubscribe that lasts 60 days. Opt-outs are honoured within 10 business days and logged. |
| F21 | Electronic Commerce Protection Regulations (CRTC), SOR/2012-36, s. 2(1)(d) ([text](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-2012-36/FullText.html)) | A commercial electronic message must set out "the mailing address, and either a telephone number providing access to an agent or a voice messaging system, an email address or a web address" of the sender | Every outreach email carries a mailing address. If the business runs from home, set up a separate mailing address (a box or mail service) before outreach starts, so the home address isn't sent out. Whether a given box qualifies: `[LEGAL REVIEW]`. That address is for email identification only; a Business Profile still can't use an unstaffed address (F3). |
