ABOUT CLEARGLASS

Transparent Intelligence
for the Public Sector

ClearGlass Inc. is a Canadian cybersecurity intelligence company headquartered in Burlington, Ontario. We design, build, and deploy production-ready intelligence platforms that give municipalities, public utilities, and government agencies complete operational visibility into their threat landscape.

Our platforms integrate open-source intelligence feeds, real-time analytics, social media monitoring, and compliance frameworks into unified command dashboards — eliminating blind spots and enabling faster, smarter decision-making across every level of municipal operations.

From OSINT collection to NIST compliance mapping, ClearGlass provides the tools that public sector security teams need to detect, analyze, and neutralize threats before they escalate.

Desmond Otieno

COO & FOUNDER — CLEARGLASS INC.

Desmond Otieno is the founder and Chief Operating Officer of ClearGlass Inc., a Canadian cybersecurity intelligence firm based in Burlington, Ontario. He architects enterprise-grade intelligence platforms, unified command dashboards, and OSINT analytics systems that serve municipal governments and public sector organizations across Canada.

His technical expertise spans full-stack development, OSINT/threat intelligence, PowerShell automation, real-time data visualization, and cybersecurity compliance framework implementation. Desmond builds production-ready systems that integrate open data APIs, social media analytics, and advanced visualization (D3.js, Chart.js, Web Audio API) into deployable single-file platforms.

Under his leadership, ClearGlass has developed the ARTEMIS IV unified command dashboard, the NEXUS OSINT intelligence platform, and a comprehensive Social Media Analytics suite — all purpose-built for the unique threat models and regulatory requirements of Canadian municipal infrastructure.
