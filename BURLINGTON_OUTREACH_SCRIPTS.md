# ClearGlass Inc. — Burlington Partnership Outreach Scripts

These are drafts, and nothing here is sent automatically. Before each send, the owner:

1. Reads the organization's official page (URL in `local_opportunity_map.json`). Then uses the contact route that page publishes for this kind of request, and saves that page's URL with the send record (CASL s. 10(9)(b), fact F17).
2. Checks the page carries no "no solicitations" statement, and the suppression list has no entry for the recipient.
3. Fills every bracket. A draft with an unfilled bracket is not ready.
4. Sends one message to one recipient: no bulk sends, no scraped addresses, no automated DMs. At most one follow-up, and only if no reply arrives.

Every email signature needs a mailing address (SOR/2012-36, s. 2(1)(d), fact F21) and an unsubscribe line that works for 60 days, with opt-outs honoured within 10 business days (fact F20). If the business runs from home, get a separate mailing address first `[DECISION REQUIRED]`.

The scripts below are plain text, ready to paste: no formatting, icons or images.

## Send record (one per message)

Recipient organization; the official page URL where the contact route was published; consent basis (F17, F18 or F19); suppression check result; the exact text sent; approver; send date; reply or opt-out and its date. Stop on an opt-out, a complaint, a negative reply, a wrong recipient, or after the single follow-up.
