# Conflict of interest and related-party policy (draft)

> **Status notice.** ClearGlass Institute is a proposed organization. It is not yet incorporated and is not a registered charity. Files in this folder are working drafts and templates, not legal advice. Signed originals belong in the minute book, not in this repository.

For adoption by the board at the organizational meeting. Not yet adopted.

## Why this policy exists

The founder of ClearGlass Institute also controls ClearGlass Inc., a for-profit company that sells cybersecurity and AI advisory services. The two share part of a name. A funder, a regulator or a journalist will ask one question first: is the Institute a marketing arm of the company? This policy is how the Institute shows that it is not, with records.

## 1. Who and what it covers

1.1 **Covered people:** every director, officer, employee and volunteer with decision-making authority.

1.2 **Related party** means:

- ClearGlass Inc. and any company it controls or is controlled by;
- a covered person, their spouse or partner, parent, child, sibling, or household member;
- any business in which a covered person or one of those family members holds more than 10 per cent, or is a director or officer.

1.3 **Conflict** means any situation where a covered person's own interest, or a related party's, could affect or appear to affect their judgment for the Institute.

## 2. Standing disclosures

2.1 Each covered person signs a disclosure when they join and every year after, listing their related parties.

2.2 The founder's interest in ClearGlass Inc. is a standing disclosed conflict. It is recorded in the minutes of the organizational meeting and does not need to be declared again at each meeting, but section 3 applies every time.

## 3. When a matter involves a related party

3.1 The conflicted person tells the board before the matter is discussed.

3.2 The conflicted person leaves the meeting for the discussion and the vote, and is not counted in the vote.

3.3 The remaining directors may approve the matter only if they record in the minutes:

- what the transaction is and who the related party is;
- why the Institute needs it;
- what the alternatives were, with at least two comparison quotes or a written reason none could be had;
- that the terms are at or below fair market value for the Institute.

3.4 If the remaining directors are too few to form a quorum, the matter waits until the board has enough independent directors, or goes to the members as the Act allows.

3.5 The Secretary keeps a related-party register listing each such transaction, its date, value, and minute reference.

## 4. Rules specific to ClearGlass Inc.

4.1 **Money.** No shared bank account. No money moves between the Institute and ClearGlass Inc. except under a written agreement approved under section 3. Money from ClearGlass Inc. to the Institute is recorded as a donation, a sponsorship with disclosed terms, or a loan with written terms. It is never undocumented start-up capital.

4.2 **Services.** The Institute does not buy services from ClearGlass Inc. unless section 3 is met. ClearGlass Inc. may give the Institute services free of charge; the gift is recorded in the minutes with an estimate of its value.

4.3 **Referrals.** The Institute does not refer anyone to ClearGlass Inc., collect leads for it, or recommend its products. Institute publications do not link to ClearGlass Inc. sales pages.

4.4 **Intellectual property.** Material the Institute produces for public education is released under CC BY 4.0 or a similar open licence. The Institute does not give ClearGlass Inc., or anyone else, an exclusive licence to it. ClearGlass Inc. keeps its own product IP; if the Institute uses any of it, the permission is in writing and free.

4.5 **People.** A person who works for both keeps a simple time record showing which organization each block of work was for. The Institute pays nothing for time spent on ClearGlass Inc. work.

4.6 **Name and brand.** Every public statement uses the full name ClearGlass Institute. The Institute's site states that it is separate from ClearGlass Inc. The Institute does not use ClearGlass Inc.'s domain, logo lockups, or sales slogans.

4.7 **Data.** The Institute does not share contact lists, workshop attendance, or any personal information with ClearGlass Inc.

## 5. Gifts

A covered person does not accept a gift worth more than $50 from anyone doing or seeking business with the Institute, and reports any gift to the Secretary.

## 6. Breach

A suspected breach is reported to the Chair, or to the Treasurer if the Chair is involved. The independent directors review it, record the outcome in the minutes, and may require a transaction to be undone at the related party's cost.

## 7. Review

The board reviews this policy every year at the first meeting after the annual meeting of members, and reports the number and total value of related-party transactions to the members.
