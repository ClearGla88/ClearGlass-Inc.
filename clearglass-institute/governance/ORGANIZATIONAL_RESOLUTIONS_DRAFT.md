# Organizational meeting: agenda and resolutions (draft)

> **Status notice.** ClearGlass Institute is a proposed organization. It is not yet incorporated and is not a registered charity. Files in this folder are working drafts and templates, not legal advice. Signed originals belong in the minute book, not in this repository.

For the first meeting of the directors after the certificate of incorporation is issued, followed the same day by the first meeting of members. Nothing here is signed or in force.

Hold both meetings within 60 days of the incorporation date, so the Institute's own by-laws are in place before the default ones apply [L-18] and the initial return is filed on time [L-12].

## Part A. Directors' meeting

**Agenda**

1. Record who is present and that a quorum is present.
2. Table the certificate and articles of incorporation.
3. Table each director's signed consent to act [L-03].
4. Record the founder's standing interest in ClearGlass Inc.
5. Pass the resolutions below.
6. Set the dates of the next four board meetings and the first annual meeting of members [L-15].

**Resolutions**

RESOLVED that:

1. **By-laws.** By-law No. 1, in the form presented to this meeting, is made a by-law of the Corporation and is submitted to the members for confirmation [L-17].
2. **Officers.** [Name] is appointed Chair, [Name] is appointed Treasurer, and [Name] is appointed Secretary, each to hold office at the pleasure of the board.
3. **Registered office.** The registered office of the Corporation is confirmed at the address in Burlington, Ontario, set out in the articles.
4. **Financial year.** The financial year of the Corporation ends on [day and month] in each year.
5. **Members.** The first directors are admitted as the first members of the Corporation.
6. **Conflict policy.** The Conflict of Interest and Related-Party Policy presented to this meeting is adopted.
7. **Banking.** The Corporation opens an account in the name ClearGlass Institute at [financial institution]. The signing officers are the Chair and the Treasurer. Any payment over $500 needs both signatures. No signing officer signs a payment to themselves or to a party related to them. The Corporation holds no account jointly with any other person or corporation.
8. **Donation receipts.** The Corporation does not issue official donation receipts unless and until the Canada Revenue Agency registers it as a charity [L-20].
9. **ClearGlass Inc.** Any dealing between the Corporation and ClearGlass Inc. requires a written agreement, the withdrawal of each conflicted director from the discussion and vote, and approval by the remaining directors recorded in the minutes.
10. **Filings.** The Secretary is directed to file the initial return within 60 days after the date of incorporation [L-12] and to keep the filing calendar.
11. **Business number.** The Treasurer is directed to confirm whether the Corporation has a business number and, if it has none, to register for one [L-26].
12. **Minute book.** The Secretary is directed to open the minute book and the registers of directors, officers and members.
13. **Public statements.** Every public statement of the Corporation states that it is not a registered charity and that donations are not tax-receiptable, until that changes.
