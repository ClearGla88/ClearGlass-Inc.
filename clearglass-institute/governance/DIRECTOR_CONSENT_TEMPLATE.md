# Consent to act as a director (template)

> **Status notice.** ClearGlass Institute is a proposed organization. It is not yet incorporated and is not a registered charity. Files in this folder are working drafts and templates, not legal advice. Signed originals belong in the minute book, not in this repository.

One per director. Sign on paper or by a signing service, keep the signed copy in the minute book, and never commit a completed copy here. This repository is public.

A person is not a director without their consent [L-03]. Collect all three before the articles are filed.

---

**To:** ClearGlass Institute (a corporation to be incorporated under the Not-for-Profit Corporations Act, 2010)

I consent to act as a first director of the Corporation, and to continue as a director until I resign, am removed, or my term ends.

I confirm that:

1. I am an individual and am at least 18 years old.
2. I have not been found by a court, in Canada or elsewhere, to be incapable, and I have not been found incapable of managing property under Ontario law.
3. I do not have the status of bankrupt.
4. I have read the Corporation's draft purposes and the Conflict of Interest and Related-Party Policy.
5. I will tell the Corporation promptly if any of statements 1 to 3 stops being true.
