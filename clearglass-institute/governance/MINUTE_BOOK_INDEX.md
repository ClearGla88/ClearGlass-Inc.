# Minute book index

> **Status notice.** ClearGlass Institute is a proposed organization. It is not yet incorporated and is not a registered charity. Files in this folder are working drafts and templates, not legal advice. Signed originals belong in the minute book, not in this repository.

The minute book is the Institute's legal record. It lives at the registered office, on paper or in a private, access-controlled folder. It does not live in this repository, which is public.

What this repository holds is the index below and, in `data/status.json`, a one-line pointer to where each record is kept.

## Register columns

**Directors:** full name, address for service, date became a director, date ceased, date of consent.

**Officers:** full name, office held, date appointed, date ceased.

**Members:** full name, address for service, class, date admitted, date ceased.

**Related-party transactions:** date, related party, what it was, value, directors who withdrew, minute reference.

## What may be written in this repository

| Allowed | Not allowed |
|---|---|
| That a record exists, its date, and which tab it is in | The record itself |
| The corporation number, once issued (it is public) | Home addresses, phone numbers, email addresses |
| Counts and dollar totals the board has approved for release | Signatures, identification, bank details, the business number letter |

The checker rejects evidence text in `data/status.json` that looks like an email address, phone number or postal code. That is a safety net, not permission to get close to the line.

## Who keeps it

The Secretary. When a director, officer or the registered office changes, the Secretary updates the register the same day and files the notice of change [L-14].
