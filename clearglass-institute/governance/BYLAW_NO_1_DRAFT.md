# By-law No. 1 (draft)

> **Status notice.** ClearGlass Institute is a proposed organization. It is not yet incorporated and is not a registered charity. Files in this folder are working drafts and templates, not legal advice. Signed originals belong in the minute book, not in this repository.

A general by-law for ClearGlass Institute. Draft for a lawyer's review. It has not been adopted, and it cannot be until the corporation exists.

Two facts shape how to use it:

- If no by-laws are passed within 60 days after incorporation, the Ministry's standard organizational by-laws apply [L-18]. Adopting the Ministry's standard by-laws on purpose, and adding only sections 6, 8 and 9 below as a short second by-law, is a sound and cheaper route. Ask counsel which to take.
- A by-law the directors pass lasts only until the next meeting of members unless the members confirm it [L-17]. Where the three directors are also the only members, hold the members' meeting the same day.

Where this draft and the Act disagree, the Act governs. Points marked CONFIRM were written from general knowledge and were not checked against the Act.

---

## 1. Interpretation

1.1 "Act" means the Not-for-Profit Corporations Act, 2010 (Ontario) and its regulations. "Corporation" means ClearGlass Institute. "Board" means the board of directors.

1.2 Words defined in the Act have the same meaning here.

## 2. Members

2.1 There is one class of members. Each member has one vote.

2.2 A person becomes a member by applying in writing and being approved by the Board. The Board admits only individuals who support the purposes of the Corporation.

2.3 The first directors are the first members.

2.4 Membership is not transferable. It ends when the member dies, resigns in writing, or is removed under 2.5.

2.5 The Board may suspend or remove a member for conduct that harms the Corporation, after giving the member 15 days' written notice of the reasons and a chance to respond in writing or in person. CONFIRM the Act's requirements for discipline of members.

2.6 No dues are payable unless the members set them by ordinary resolution.

## 3. Meetings of members

3.1 The annual meeting is held each year on a date the Board sets, within the time the Act requires [L-15]. The Board plans it within six months after the financial year end [L-16].

3.2 At each annual meeting the members receive the financial statements, elect directors, and appoint an auditor or person to conduct a review engagement, or pass the resolution the Act permits in place of that appointment [L-06].

3.3 Notice of a meeting of members is given to each member entitled to vote, to the directors, and to the auditor if any, not less than 10 and not more than 50 days before the meeting. CONFIRM the notice period.

3.4 A majority of the members entitled to vote, present in person or by any means the Act permits, is a quorum.

3.5 Meetings may be held by telephone or electronic means that let everyone taking part communicate with each other at the same time.

3.6 Questions are decided by a majority of the votes cast, unless the Act or the articles require more. The chair of the meeting has no second or casting vote.

3.7 A member may vote by proxy only if and as the Act permits. CONFIRM the Act's proxy rules.

## 4. Directors

4.1 The Board manages or supervises the management of the Corporation.

4.2 The number of directors is at least 3 and at most 9, as the articles provide [L-01]. Within that range the members fix the number by special resolution, or authorize the Board to fix it. CONFIRM.

4.3 Every director must be an individual who is at least 18, is not bankrupt, and has not been found incapable [L-02]. A director need not be a member. CONFIRM.

4.4 A person is not a director until they consent in writing [L-03].

4.5 Members elect directors at the annual meeting for a term of two years. A director may be re-elected. CONFIRM the maximum term the Act allows.

4.6 The members may remove a director by ordinary resolution at a special meeting. A vacancy is filled as the Act provides.

4.7 Directors are not paid for acting as directors. Reasonable expenses are reimbursed on receipts.

4.8 If the Corporation is a public benefit corporation, the Board keeps the number of directors who are employees of the Corporation or its affiliates within the Act's limit [L-05].

## 5. Meetings of directors

5.1 The Board meets at least four times a year.

5.2 Notice of a Board meeting is given at least 7 days before the meeting, or 48 hours for an urgent matter. A director may waive notice.

5.3 A majority of the directors in office is a quorum.

5.4 Each director has one vote. The chair has no second or casting vote. A tie defeats the motion.

5.5 Directors may meet by telephone or electronic means, and may act by a resolution in writing signed by all directors entitled to vote on it.

## 6. Officers

6.1 The Board appoints a Chair, a Treasurer and a Secretary from among the directors, and may appoint an Executive Director.

6.2 The Chair presides at meetings of the Board and of members.

6.3 The Treasurer keeps the accounting records, reports to each Board meeting on receipts, payments and balances, and makes sure tax and information returns are filed on time.

6.4 The Secretary keeps the minute book and the registers, gives notices, and files the returns and notices the registry requires [L-12] [L-13] [L-14].

6.5 Once the Corporation holds a grant or has applied for charitable registration, the same person does not serve as both Chair and Executive Director.

6.6 Officers hold office at the pleasure of the Board.

## 7. Conflict of interest

7.1 A director or officer who has an interest in a contract or transaction with the Corporation discloses it as the Act requires, does not attend the part of the meeting where it is discussed, and does not vote on it.

7.2 The Board adopts and follows the Conflict of Interest and Related-Party Policy. That policy applies to every dealing with ClearGlass Inc.

## 8. Finance

8.1 The financial year ends on the date the Board fixes by resolution.

8.2 The Corporation's money is kept in accounts in the Corporation's own name. No account is shared with any other person or corporation.

8.3 Two signing officers approve any payment over $500. No signing officer approves a payment to themselves or to a party related to them.

8.4 The Corporation does not borrow, lend, or give a guarantee without a Board resolution.

8.5 The Corporation does not issue official donation receipts unless it is a registered charity [L-20].

## 9. Records and separation

9.1 The Corporation keeps at its registered office the records the Act requires, including the articles, by-laws, minutes, and registers of directors, officers and members.

9.2 The Corporation's records, accounts, domain, and public communications are kept separate from those of ClearGlass Inc.

## 10. Protection of directors and officers

10.1 The Corporation indemnifies its directors and officers to the extent, and on the conditions, the Act permits.

10.2 The Corporation may buy insurance for its directors and officers as the Act permits.

## 11. Notices

11.1 A notice may be given in person, by mail, or by email to the address in the Corporation's records.

## 12. Amendment

12.1 The Board may make, amend or repeal a by-law. The change takes effect when the Board passes it and is put to the members at their next meeting, where they may confirm, reject or amend it [L-17].

12.2 A change the Act reserves to the members by special resolution takes effect only when the members pass it.
