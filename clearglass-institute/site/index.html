ClearGlass Institute

Clarity in the public interest.

ClearGlass Institute is a proposed organization. It is not yet incorporated and is not a registered charity. It does not accept donations, payments or memberships.

What it is for

The aim is to advance public digital safety, AI literacy, and knowledge of how information systems work, so that individuals, small organizations and communities can understand and reduce digital risk without relying on vendor marketing.

Planned work

  • Short public briefs on topics such as phishing, AI model risk, data exposure, and the ethics of open-source research. Free to read, with sources cited.
  • Open methodology cards on defensive security practice, published as educational material.
  • Workshops for students and community groups, announced only once a host organization has agreed in writing.

Nothing has been published yet.

Proposed purposes

  1. To advance public education in cybersecurity, artificial intelligence literacy, digital investigations literacy, and information integrity.
  2. To research, document, and publish non-commercial frameworks that help the public understand digital risk, system transparency, and defensive security practices.
  3. To convene educational programs, workshops, and open resources for students, community organizations, and the general public in Canada.
  4. To collaborate with educators, researchers, and civil-society groups on public-interest digital safety initiatives.

These are draft purposes. They may change on legal advice before anything is filed.

Separate from ClearGlass Inc.

ClearGlass Institute is planned as a body separate from ClearGlass Inc., a for-profit company. The Institute will not sell products or services, will not share funds or bank accounts with the company, and will not refer anyone to it.
