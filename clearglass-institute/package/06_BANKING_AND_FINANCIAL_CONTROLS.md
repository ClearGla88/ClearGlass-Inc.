# 6. Banking and financial controls

> **Status notice.** ClearGlass Institute is a proposed organization. It has not been incorporated. ClearGlass Institute is not currently a registered charity with the Canada Revenue Agency. Donations are not eligible for official charitable tax receipts. Documents in this folder are drafts and templates, not legal advice. Signed originals belong in the minute book, not in this repository.

Resolutions, policies and checklists for the Institute's money. None is in force. The resolutions are passed at the first directors' meeting; the policies are adopted by the board. [LEGAL REVIEW] before adoption.

**The rule that governs everything here.** ClearGlass Inc. funds must not be transferred to the Institute as "capital". Money or services from ClearGlass Inc. reach the Institute only as a documented, board-approved donation, grant or sponsorship, or as a loan on written fair-market terms. Each needs a written agreement, the withdrawal of every director with an interest, and a resolution of the remaining directors recorded in the minutes [L-32].

## 6.1 Bank-account opening resolution

RESOLVED that:

1. The Corporation open and maintain an account in the name **ClearGlass Institute** at [NAME OF FINANCIAL INSTITUTION] (the "Bank").
2. The account be used only for the Corporation's own money. No account of the Corporation is held jointly with, or in the name of, any other person or corporation, including ClearGlass Inc.
3. The Chair and the Treasurer are authorized to sign the Bank's account-opening documents and to give the Bank a certified copy of this resolution and of the signing-authority resolution.
4. The Treasurer report to the board at its next meeting that the account is open, and file the account-opening documents in the minute book.

[CONFIRM] with the chosen Bank which documents it requires from a newly incorporated not-for-profit corporation.

## 6.2 Signing-authority resolution

RESOLVED that:

1. The signing officers of the Corporation are the Chair and the Treasurer. The board may add or remove a signing officer by resolution.
2. Any single payment, transfer, withdrawal or commitment of **more than $500** requires authorization by **two** signing officers.
3. A payment of $500 or less may be authorized by one signing officer, and is reported to the board in the Treasurer's next report.
4. No signing officer authorizes a payment to themselves, to a member of their family or household, or to a party related to them. Such a payment is authorized by two other signing officers, or by one other signing officer and a director who has no interest in it.
5. A payment may not be split into smaller amounts to avoid the limit in paragraph 2.
6. Online banking access is set up so that the two-person rule in paragraph 2 is enforced by the Bank's system where the Bank offers that feature. [CONFIRM] with the Bank.
7. The Secretary notify the Bank in writing within 5 business days of any change in the signing officers.

## 6.3 Expense-approval policy

| Amount | Within the approved budget | Outside the approved budget |
|---|---|---|
| $500 or less | One signing officer | Board resolution first |
| More than $500 and up to [LIMIT] | Two signing officers | Board resolution first |
| More than [LIMIT] | Board resolution first, then two signing officers | Board resolution first |
| Any amount, to a related party | Board resolution under the conflict policy, then two signing officers who have no interest | Same |

[DECISION REQUIRED] the upper limit above which the board must approve even budgeted spending.

Rules:

1. Every payment is supported by an invoice or receipt naming the Institute.
2. Expenses are reimbursed only on original receipts, within 60 days, and never for a director's or officer's time.
3. The person who incurs an expense does not approve it.
4. Recurring commitments (subscriptions, hosting, insurance) are approved once by the board with an annual cost and reviewed each year.
5. The Treasurer reports all payments since the last meeting at each board meeting.

## 6.4 Prohibited-transactions policy

The Institute does not:

1. distribute any surplus, or pay a dividend, bonus or share of revenue, to a member, director or officer;
2. pay a director for acting as a director;
3. make a loan to, or guarantee an obligation of, a director, officer, member, or related party;
4. accept money from, or pay money to, ClearGlass Inc. except under the conflict policy and the rule at the top of this document;
5. pay an expense of ClearGlass Inc., or let ClearGlass Inc. pay an expense of the Institute, without a written agreement approved under the conflict policy;
6. issue any receipt that states or implies a charitable tax benefit while the Institute is not a registered charity [L-20];
7. accept a gift on a condition that would require the Institute to promote a product or service;
8. pay or receive a commission, referral fee or finder's fee;
9. hold or move money through a personal account, or an account of any other organization;
10. make a cash payment, or a payment with no supporting document;
11. invest its money other than in an insured deposit account without a board resolution. [LEGAL REVIEW] the investment rules that apply to a charitable corporation.

## 6.5 Related-party transaction approval checklist

Complete before any contract or payment involving ClearGlass Inc. or another related party. File the completed checklist with the minutes.

- [ ] The related party and each director's or officer's interest in it are disclosed in writing and entered in the minutes
- [ ] Every director with an interest left the meeting for the discussion and the vote
- [ ] The remaining directors, or the members if every director has an interest, passed a resolution approving it
- [ ] The minutes state why the Institute needs the transaction
- [ ] At least two comparison quotes are attached, or the minutes explain why none could be obtained
- [ ] The minutes state why the terms are no less favourable to the Institute than fair market value
- [ ] A written agreement signed by both parties is attached
- [ ] If money comes from ClearGlass Inc., the agreement states whether it is a donation, grant, sponsorship or loan, and a loan states its interest rate, term and repayment schedule
- [ ] If intellectual property is involved, the agreement keeps public-education material available for public-benefit use and grants no exclusive licence over it
- [ ] The payment is authorized by two signing officers who have no interest in it
- [ ] The transaction is entered in the related-party register

## 6.7 Bookkeeping checklist

**Every transaction**

- [ ] Invoice or receipt saved, naming the Institute
- [ ] Entered in the accounting record with date, payee or payer, amount, purpose and budget line
- [ ] Authorization recorded (who, and the second person where required)
- [ ] Related-party transactions flagged and entered in the related-party register

**Every month**

- [ ] Bank statement reconciled to the accounting record by someone other than the person who made the payments
- [ ] Unusual or unsupported items reported to the Treasurer

**Every quarter**

- [ ] Statement of receipts, payments and balances given to the board [L-36]
- [ ] Budget-to-actual comparison given to the board
- [ ] Outside donations and government grants totalled for the year to date [L-04]

**Every year**

- [ ] Financial statements prepared and approved by the board, signed by at least one director [L-16]
- [ ] Audit, review engagement, or the permitted resolution in place of them [L-06]
- [ ] Federal income tax return, and the non-profit information return if its tests are met [L-25] [L-24]
- [ ] Records retained. [CONFIRM] the retention period with the Institute's accountant
