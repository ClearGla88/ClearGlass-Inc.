# 3. General operating by-law (first draft)

> **Status notice.** ClearGlass Institute is a proposed organization. It has not been incorporated. ClearGlass Institute is not currently a registered charity with the Canada Revenue Agency. Donations are not eligible for official charitable tax receipts. Documents in this folder are drafts and templates, not legal advice. Signed originals belong in the minute book, not in this repository.

By-law No. 1 of ClearGlass Institute. A first draft for a lawyer's review. It has not been made, and cannot be until the corporation exists.

How it takes effect: the directors make it at their first meeting, it is effective from that resolution, and the members confirm it at their next meeting. If the directors pass no organizational by-law within 60 days of incorporation, the Ministry's standard by-laws apply instead. The legal basis for each article is in the drafting notes at the end, kept out of the text so the by-law can be copied clean.

Where this by-law and the Act disagree, the Act governs. [LEGAL REVIEW] the whole document before it is made.

## BY-LAW NO. 1

A by-law relating generally to the conduct of the affairs of ClearGlass Institute.

### Article 1. Interpretation

1.1 In this by-law, "Act" means the Not-for-Profit Corporations Act, 2010 (Ontario) and its regulations; "Corporation" means ClearGlass Institute; "Board" means the board of directors; "related party" has the meaning given in the Corporation's Conflict-of-Interest and Related-Party Policy.

1.2 Words defined in the Act have the same meaning in this by-law.

1.3 If a provision of this by-law conflicts with the Act or the articles, the Act or the articles prevail.

### Article 2. Registered office

2.1 The registered office of the Corporation is in the City of Burlington, Ontario, at the address the articles or a later resolution specifies.

2.2 The Corporation keeps the records the Act requires at its registered office.

### Article 3. Purposes and non-profit restriction

3.1 The Corporation carries on its activities only to advance the purposes set out in its articles.

3.2 The Corporation is carried on without the purpose of gain for its members, directors, officers or related parties. Any surplus is used to promote its purposes.

3.3 No part of the Corporation's income or property is paid to, or made available for the personal benefit of, a member, director, officer or related party, except as the Act, the law relating to charities, and Article 9 allow.

### Article 4. Members

4.1 **One class.** The Corporation has one class of members. Each member has one vote at every meeting of members.

4.2 **Eligibility.** A member must be an individual who is at least 18 years old and who supports the purposes of the Corporation. No corporation or other entity may be a member.

4.3 **Admission.** A person becomes a member by applying in writing and being admitted by resolution of the Board. The first directors are the first members.

4.4 **Rights.** A member may receive notice of, attend and vote at meetings of members, and has the other rights the Act gives a member.

4.5 **No transfer.** A membership cannot be transferred.

4.6 **Resignation.** A member may resign by written notice to the Secretary. The resignation takes effect when the notice is received, or at a later time stated in it.

4.7 **Termination.** A membership ends when the member dies, resigns, or is removed under 4.8.

4.8 **Removal.** The Board may suspend or remove a member who has materially breached the articles, the by-laws or a written policy of the Corporation, or whose conduct is harmful to the Corporation. Before deciding, the Board gives the member at least 15 days' written notice of the proposed action and its reasons, and a chance to respond in writing or in person. The Board decides in good faith and in a fair and reasonable manner, and gives its decision in writing.

4.9 **Dues.** No dues are payable unless the members set them by ordinary resolution.

### Article 5. Meetings of members

5.1 **Annual meeting.** The Board calls an annual meeting of members within 18 months after incorporation and afterwards not later than 15 months after the preceding annual meeting, and in each year not later than six months after the end of the financial year.

5.2 **Business.** At each annual meeting the members receive the financial statements and the report on them, elect directors, and appoint an auditor or a person to conduct a review engagement, or pass the resolution the Act permits in its place.

5.3 **Special meetings.** The Board may call a special meeting of members at any time, and must call one when the Act requires.

5.4 **Notice.** Notice of the time and place of a meeting of members is given not less than 10 and not more than 50 days before the meeting to each member, each director, and the auditor or person appointed to conduct a review engagement.

5.5 **Place and electronic meetings.** Meetings of members are held in Ontario at a place the Board chooses, or entirely or partly by telephonic or electronic means that let everyone taking part communicate adequately with each other.

5.6 **Quorum.** A majority of the members entitled to vote is a quorum.

5.7 **Voting.** Questions are decided by a majority of the votes cast unless the Act, the articles or this by-law require more. The chair has no second or casting vote. A tie defeats the motion.

5.8 **No proxies.** Members vote in person or by the telephonic or electronic means in use at the meeting. Voting by proxy is not permitted. [DECISION REQUIRED] whether to permit proxies.

5.9 **Written resolutions.** A resolution signed by all the members entitled to vote on it is as valid as if passed at a meeting.

### Article 6. Board of directors

6.1 **Role.** The Board manages or supervises the management of the activities and affairs of the Corporation.

6.2 **Number.** The Board has at least 3 and at most 9 directors. Within that range the number is fixed by special resolution of the members, or by the Board if a special resolution empowers it.

6.3 **Qualifications.** A director must be an individual who is at least 18 years old, is not bankrupt, and has not been found incapable as described in the Act. A director need not be a member.

6.4 **Consent.** A person elected or appointed as a director must consent in writing before, or within 10 days after, the election or appointment.

6.5 **Composition.** Not more than one-third of the directors may be employees of the Corporation or of any of its affiliates. At least one director must have no financial interest in, and hold no office or employment with, ClearGlass Inc. [DECISION REQUIRED] whether to require a majority of such directors.

6.6 **Election and term.** The members elect directors by ordinary resolution at the first meeting of members and at each annual meeting where an election is due. Directors are elected for a term ending at the close of the second annual meeting after their election, and may be re-elected.

6.7 **Removal.** The members may remove a director by ordinary resolution at a special meeting called for that purpose.

6.8 **Vacancies.** A quorum of directors may fill a vacancy for the rest of the vacated term, except where the Act requires the members to fill it.

6.9 **Ceasing to hold office.** A director ceases to hold office on death, resignation, removal, or ceasing to be qualified.

6.10 **No remuneration.** Directors are not paid for acting as directors. Reasonable expenses incurred for the Corporation are reimbursed on receipts. [LEGAL REVIEW] any payment to a director in another capacity.

### Article 7. Meetings of directors

7.1 **Frequency.** The Board meets at least four times in each financial year.

7.2 **Calling and notice.** The Chair or any two directors may call a meeting on at least 7 days' notice, or 48 hours' notice for an urgent matter. A director may waive notice. Attendance is a waiver unless the director attends to object that the meeting was not properly called.

7.3 **Quorum.** A majority of the directors in office is a quorum.

7.4 **Voting.** Each director has one vote. Questions are decided by a majority of the votes cast. The chair has no second or casting vote. A tie defeats the motion.

7.5 **Electronic participation.** A director may take part in a meeting by telephonic or electronic means that let everyone taking part communicate adequately with each other, and is then present at the meeting.

7.6 **Written resolutions.** A resolution signed by all the directors entitled to vote on it is as valid as if passed at a meeting.

7.7 **No alternates.** No person may act for an absent director.

### Article 8. Officers

8.1 **Offices.** The Board appoints a Chair, a Treasurer and a Secretary from among the directors. The Board may appoint an Executive Director, who need not be a director.

8.2 **Chair.** The Chair presides at meetings of the Board and of members and sees that the Board's decisions are carried out.

8.3 **Treasurer.** The Treasurer keeps the accounting records, reports on the Corporation's financial position at each Board meeting, and sees that tax and information returns are filed on time.

8.4 **Secretary.** The Secretary keeps the minute book and the registers, gives the notices this by-law requires, and files the returns and notices of change the registry requires.

8.5 **Executive Director.** The Executive Director manages the day-to-day activities of the Corporation under the Board's direction and reports to the Board at each meeting.

8.6 **Separation of roles.** The same person may not be both Chair and Executive Director. [DECISION REQUIRED] whether this applies from the first meeting or from the first grant or charity application.

8.7 **Tenure.** Officers hold office at the pleasure of the Board.

### Article 9. Duties, conflicts of interest and related parties

9.1 **Standard of care.** Every director and officer acts honestly and in good faith with a view to the best interests of the Corporation, and exercises the care, diligence and skill that a reasonably prudent person would exercise in comparable circumstances.

9.2 **Disclosure.** A director or officer who is a party to a material contract or transaction, or proposed one, with the Corporation, or who is a director or officer of, or has a material interest in, a party to one, discloses the nature and extent of the interest as the Act requires and has it entered in the minutes.

9.3 **Withdrawal.** A director with an interest described in 9.2 does not attend any part of a meeting of directors during which the contract or transaction is discussed and does not vote on it, except where the Act permits.

9.4 **Related-party transactions.** The Corporation enters into a contract or transaction with a related party, including ClearGlass Inc., only if the directors who have no interest in it approve it, on written terms, after recording in the minutes why the Corporation needs it and why its terms are no less favourable to the Corporation than fair market value. If every director has an interest, only the members may approve it.

9.5 **Policy.** The Board adopts and follows a Conflict-of-Interest and Related-Party Policy and reviews it each year.

### Article 10. Financial controls

10.1 **Financial year.** The financial year of the Corporation ends on the date the Board fixes by resolution. [DECISION REQUIRED] the year end.

10.2 **Bank accounts.** The Corporation's money is kept in accounts in the Corporation's own legal name at a financial institution the Board approves. No account is held jointly with, or in the name of, any other person or corporation.

10.3 **Dual authorization.** Two signing officers must authorize any single payment or commitment of more than $500. No signing officer authorizes a payment to themselves or to a party related to them.

10.4 **Expenditure approval.** Spending follows the annual budget the Board approves. Spending outside the budget, and any single commitment above a limit the Board sets, needs a Board resolution first.

10.5 **Borrowing, lending and guarantees.** The Corporation does not borrow, lend or give a guarantee without a Board resolution. [LEGAL REVIEW] limits that apply to a charitable corporation.

10.6 **Records.** The Corporation keeps accounting records adequate to show its financial position with reasonable accuracy on a quarterly basis, together with the other records the Act requires.

10.7 **Annual financial review.** The members appoint an auditor or a person to conduct a review engagement at each annual meeting, unless they pass the extraordinary resolution the Act permits for a corporation of the Corporation's type and revenue.

10.8 **Donation receipts.** The Corporation does not issue receipts for income tax purposes unless and until it is registered as a charity under the Income Tax Act (Canada).

### Article 11. Indemnification

11.1 The Corporation indemnifies each director and officer, and each former director and officer, to the extent and on the conditions the Act permits.

11.2 The Corporation may buy insurance for the benefit of its directors and officers only as the Act and the law relating to charities permit. [LEGAL REVIEW] before any policy is bought.

### Article 12. Notices

12.1 A notice to a member, director or officer may be given in person, by mail, or by email to the address shown in the Corporation's records.

### Article 13. Amendment

13.1 The Board may make, amend or repeal a by-law, except on a matter the Act reserves to the members. The change is effective from the date of the Board's resolution.

13.2 The Board submits the by-law, amendment or repeal to the members at the next meeting of members. The members may confirm, reject or amend it by ordinary resolution. If it is not submitted, or is rejected, it ceases to have effect.

13.3 A change the Act requires to be made by special resolution takes effect only when the members pass it.

### Article 14. Dissolution and distribution of property

14.1 The Corporation may be dissolved as the Act provides.

14.2 On dissolution, after all debts and liabilities are paid, the remaining property of the Corporation is distributed as its articles provide and never to its members.

### Article 15. Effective date

15.1 This by-law takes effect when the Board makes it.
