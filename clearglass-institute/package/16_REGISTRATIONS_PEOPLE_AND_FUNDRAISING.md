# 16. Registrations, people and fundraising

> **Status notice.** ClearGlass Institute is a proposed organization. It has not been incorporated. ClearGlass Institute is not currently a registered charity with the Canada Revenue Agency. Donations are not eligible for official charitable tax receipts. Documents in this folder are drafts and templates, not legal advice. Signed originals belong in the minute book, not in this repository.

This document covers phases 5, 7 and 8 of the owner's prompt of 2026-11-15. Most registrations are set off by an event, not by a date: the first employee, the first fee, the first fundraising email. Each row below names its trigger. Until the trigger happens, the registration is not needed, and nobody should register early just to tick a box.

Each row is labelled with what it rests on: a rule id from the register, or [CONFIRM] where no source was read for this package.

## 16.2 People

### Who is who

| Role | What decides their status | Paid? | Paperwork before they start | Rules |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Director | Elected or appointed, with written consent | No, not for acting as a director | Consent; conflict disclosure; commitment letter | [L-02] [L-03] [L-30] [L-39] |
| Officer | Appointed by the directors | Only as the board approves and charity law permits | Appointment resolution; conflict disclosure | [L-30] [L-38] |
| Employee | Paid to work under the Institute's direction; the Employment Standards Act applies | Yes, under the Act's standards | Written offer; payroll set-up; accessibility training | [L-49] [L-50]; one-third limit on employee-directors [L-05] |
| Volunteer | Unpaid; the Institute's benefit and the person's own view of the arrangement decide it, not the label | No. Reimbursement of approved expenses only | Volunteer agreement; confidentiality; licence of work | [L-50] |
| Intern or trainee | Generally an employee if trained in a skill the Institute's employees use, unless in a school work-experience program or an approved college or university program | Yes, unless an exemption applies | School placement agreement where one applies; otherwise as an employee | [L-50] |
| Contractor | In business for themselves | Yes, on invoice | Written agreement with assignment or licence of work; conflict declaration | [L-50]; doc 4 if connected to ClearGlass Inc. |

**Rule (recommended control).** No unpaid intern outside an approved school, college or university program. No volunteer doing work that a paid employee of the Institute does.

### Hiring checklist (first employee)

- [ ] Board resolution approving the role and the budget
- [ ] If the person is or will be a director: the one-third limit checked [L-05]
- [ ] Conflict check: any tie to ClearGlass Inc. goes through doc 4
- [ ] Written offer with duties, pay, hours and start date
- [ ] Payroll deductions account in place [CONFIRM]
- [ ] Workplace safety insurance position confirmed [CONFIRM]
- [ ] Accessibility policies adopted and training given [L-49]
- [ ] Accounts created under their own login, with multifactor authentication (risk 21)
- [ ] Personnel file kept privately, not in this repository

### Volunteer agreement: contents

Role and supervisor; unpaid status and expense rule; confidentiality; personal information rules (doc 18.1); a non-exclusive licence to the Institute of anything the volunteer creates for it, under the open licence chosen in doc 13, C3 [LEGAL REVIEW] (doc 13, C10); the AI policy (doc 18.3); how either side ends the arrangement.

### Contractor agreement: contents

Deliverables and dates; fee and invoicing; the contractor supplies their own tools and controls how the work is done; assignment of copyright, or a licence wide enough for the open licence, plus a waiver of moral rights [LEGAL REVIEW]; confidentiality; personal information rules; a declaration of any tie to ClearGlass Inc., with doc 4 applied if there is one; termination.

### Director commitment letter: contents

Time expected; meetings; duty of honesty and care [L-39]; conflict disclosure each year (doc 4); confidentiality; no pay for acting as a director [L-30]; return of records on leaving. It is a statement of expectations, not a contract for services.
