## Notes on particular outcomes

**Outcome 7, briefs.** A brief counts only if every item in the publication checklist of `08_FIRST_PUBLIC_BRIEF_OUTLINE.md` is ticked. A brief that cannot be fully sourced is not published, and the count stays where it is. Quality is the measure; three is the target.

**Outcome 9, partner-outreach list.** The list is an internal working document. It records the kinds of organization to approach (for example public libraries, colleges, settlement agencies, community centres) and the criteria for choosing among them. A named organization is a prospect, not a partner. No organization is named in public, in a grant application as a confirmed host, or on the page until it has agreed in writing and is entered in `named_partners` in `data/status.json`. Contacting any organization is a person's act. [DECISION REQUIRED] who makes the approaches and in whose name.

**Outcome 12, charity decision memo.** The memo answers the question the Act raises on day one, not a question that can wait: with educational purposes the Institute may already be a charitable corporation under Ontario law [L-29]. It should cover:

1. Counsel's view on whether the Institute is a charitable corporation under the Act, and what follows [L-29] [L-30].
2. The Institute's income tax position while unregistered [L-23] [L-25].
3. What registration would allow: donation receipts, and grants from funders that require a qualified donee [L-20].
4. What registration would require: purposes and activities that qualify, ongoing reporting, limits on activities. [LEGAL REVIEW]
5. How to apply [L-21], and what is known about timing. CRA registration timelines vary and should be confirmed with the CRA or qualified counsel [L-22].
6. A recommendation, and the cost of each option.

If stage 0 advice already settles the question, the memo records that advice and the board's decision, and this outcome closes early.

## 90-day review

At day 90 the board records, for each outcome: met or not met, the evidence, and for any not met, the new date or the decision to drop it. The review also records:

- compliance incidents: target 0;
- registry and tax filings made on time: target all;
- related-party transactions without minutes: target 0;
- total outside donations and government grants to date, against the $10,000 line [L-04];
- open items remaining in `STATUS.md`.

Do not report reach or impact (people reached, organizations served) unless the figure can be counted from records the Institute holds.
