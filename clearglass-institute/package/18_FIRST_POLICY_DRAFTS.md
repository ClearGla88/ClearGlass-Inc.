## 18.1 Privacy policy

**Adopted by the board on [DATE], resolution [NUMBER].**

1. **Scope.** This policy covers personal information about any identifiable individual that the Institute collects, uses, discloses or keeps. That includes workshop attendees, supporters, donors, volunteers, contractors, staff and directors.
2. **Which law applies.** [LEGAL REVIEW] Which privacy statute governs each of the Institute's activities is open (doc 13, C6). The federal private-sector law reaches personal information handled in commercial activity, and non-profit status does not settle the question [L-51]. This policy applies whether or not a statute does.
3. **Accountability.** The Secretary is the privacy officer. Questions and complaints go to the Secretary, or to the Chair where the complaint is about the Secretary.
4. **Purpose first.** Before collecting anything, the Institute writes down why it needs it. Information is used only for that purpose, unless the person agrees to another one.
5. **Collect the minimum.** Ask only for what the stated purpose needs. Workshop sign-in uses first name and organization type, not full contact details, unless the person asks for follow-up.
6. **Consent.** Information is collected with the person's knowledge and consent. Consent to receive messages is recorded separately from attendance, with the date and the wording shown [L-48].
7. **No sharing with ClearGlass Inc.** No personal information held by the Institute is given to ClearGlass Inc., or used to contact anyone for ClearGlass Inc.'s business, under any arrangement. This includes attendee lists, supporter lists and enquiries.
8. **No sale or rental.** The Institute does not sell, rent or trade personal information.
9. **Service providers.** A provider that will hold personal information for the Institute is approved by two officers, after checking where the data is stored and how access is controlled. The Institute keeps the list of providers.
10. **AI tools.** No personal information is entered into an AI tool except as section 18.3 allows.
11. **Security.** Each person has their own login, with multifactor authentication where offered. Access is limited to those who need it and is reviewed every quarter (risk 21).
12. **Retention.** Each category has a deletion date in the retention policy (doc 15, section B). Attendance records are deleted [PERIOD] after the event. [DECISION REQUIRED] the period.
13. **Access and correction.** Anyone may ask what the Institute holds about them and ask for it to be corrected. The Secretary replies within 30 days.
14. **Breaches.** A suspected loss or exposure goes to the Chair the same day. The Chair contains it, keeps the evidence, and decides with counsel whether anyone must be notified. [LEGAL REVIEW] notification duties.
15. **This repository.** No personal information goes in the public repository.

**Public notice (short form, for the site and sign-up forms).** "ClearGlass Institute collects only the information it needs for the purpose stated on this form, uses it only for that purpose, never shares it with ClearGlass Inc. or anyone else for their own use, and deletes it on a set schedule. To see or correct what we hold about you, contact the Institute's privacy officer at [ADDRESS]."

## 18.2 Gift acceptance and donation policy

**Adopted by the board on [DATE], resolution [NUMBER].**

1. **No tax receipts.** Until the Institute is registered as a charity, it issues no receipt for income tax purposes, and no acknowledgement states or suggests a tax benefit [L-20]. Every acknowledgement carries the charity disclaimer word for word.
2. **Acknowledgement wording.** "Thank you for your gift of [AMOUNT] to ClearGlass Institute on [DATE]. ClearGlass Institute is not currently a registered charity with the Canada Revenue Agency. This is not an official donation receipt for income tax purposes." [LEGAL REVIEW]
3. **Who issues.** Only the Treasurer issues acknowledgements, and keeps a copy of each.
4. **Gifts accepted.** Money by cheque or transfer to the Institute's own account; gifts in kind that the Institute will use for its programs.
5. **Gifts refused.** A gift is refused if it:
   - comes with conditions that give the donor any say over content, research findings or who is taught;
   - asks for the donor's products or services to be promoted, or for endorsement;
   - would require the Institute to act outside its purposes;
   - comes from a source the board considers would damage public trust in the Institute; or
   - is cash above [LIMIT] that cannot be traced.
6. **Gifts from the founder or ClearGlass Inc.** Accepted only as a documented donation, grant or sponsorship approved under doc 4, and recorded in the contribution ledger (doc 14.3). Never treated as "capital" (doc 6).
7. **Restricted gifts.** Accepted only if the restriction fits the purposes and the board approves it. Restricted money is tracked separately and spent only as restricted.
8. **Anonymous gifts.** Accepted; the Treasurer still records the donor privately. The donor's name is not published without consent.
9. **Sponsorships.** Recognition by name and logo only, under a written agreement approved by the board. No exclusivity and no content control (doc 16.3, risk 22).
10. **Returning a gift.** A gift accepted in error is returned on a board resolution, and the return is recorded.
11. **Review.** The board reviews this policy before applying for charitable registration, and again on registration, when receipting rules start to apply.

## 18.3 AI use and transparency policy

**Adopted by the board on [DATE], resolution [NUMBER].**

1. **Purpose.** The Institute teaches AI governance. Its own use of AI tools has to meet the standard it teaches.
2. **A named human is responsible.** Every published sentence has a named human author, who is responsible for it whether or not a tool helped draft it (doc 8, risk 19).
3. **No source, no sentence.** Every fact, figure, quotation and citation is checked against its source by the author, and by a second reader, before publication. A source a tool suggested is treated as unverified until a person has opened it.
4. **Disclosure.** [DECISION REQUIRED] Whether drafting assistance is disclosed, and in what words (doc 13, C5). Until the board decides, each publication states it.
5. **Allowed uses.** Drafting, summarizing, editing, translation and code, on material that contains no personal or confidential information.
6. **Risk check before other uses.** Before any use that involves personal information, confidential information, or a decision about a person, the user writes down: the tool, the data, where the data goes, whether the provider keeps or trains on it, and who reviews the output. Two officers approve. No tool may make a decision about a person's access, eligibility, award or role (for example a scholarship, doc 14.4).
7. **Personal information.** No personal information goes into an AI tool unless the provider's terms keep it confidential and do not use it for training, and the privacy officer approves (doc 18.1).
8. **Accounts.** Tools are used under the Institute's own accounts, not personal or ClearGlass Inc. accounts, so that its records stay its own (risk 21).
9. **Teaching material.** Material that demonstrates attacks or weaknesses stays defensive and contains no working instructions for harm (risk 17).
10. **Errors.** A published error is corrected in place, with a dated note, and entered in the corrections log.
11. **Review.** The board reviews this policy every year, and whenever the Institute starts a new kind of AI use.
