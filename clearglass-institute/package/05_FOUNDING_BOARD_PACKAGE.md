# 5. Founding board package

## 5.5 Organizational meeting agenda

**Part A. First meeting of directors**

1. Call to order; record those present and that a quorum is present.
2. Table the certificate and articles of incorporation.
3. Table each director's signed consent to act and conflict-of-interest disclosure.
4. Record the standing disclosure of each director's interest in ClearGlass Inc.
5. Make By-law No. 1.
6. Adopt the Conflict-of-Interest and Related-Party Policy.
7. Appoint the Chair, Treasurer and Secretary.
8. Confirm the registered office.
9. Fix the financial year end.
10. Admit the first members.
11. Banking: account, signing officers, $500 dual-authorization limit.
12. Approve the first budget.
13. Direct the initial return, the business number, the minute book, and the compliance calendar.
14. Approve the public disclaimers.
15. Set the dates of the next four board meetings.
16. Adjourn.

**Part B. First meeting of members (or signed resolution)**

1. Record those present and that a quorum is present.
2. Confirm By-law No. 1.
3. Elect the directors.
4. Appoint an auditor or a person to conduct a review engagement, or pass the permitted resolution in place of that appointment.
5. Adjourn.

*The first directors' term ends at the close of the first meeting of members, so item B3 is required [L-31].*

## 5.10 Annual compliance calendar

Dates run from the incorporation date and the financial year end. Once both are recorded in `data/status.json`, `STATUS.md` lists the actual dates. To model them beforehand:

```bash
python3 clearglass-institute/tools/institute_kit.py calendar --incorporated YYYY-MM-DD --fye MM-DD
```

**Once, after incorporation**

| Due | Obligation | Owner | Rule |
|---|---|---|---|
| Within 60 days | Initial return filed with the Ontario Business Registry | Secretary | [L-12] |
| Within 60 days | By-law No. 1 made, or the standard by-laws apply | Board | [L-18] |
| First members' meeting | By-law confirmed; directors elected | Members | [L-17] [L-31] |
| Within 18 months | First annual meeting of members | Board | [L-15] |
