# Articles of incorporation: fill-in sheet

> **Status notice.** ClearGlass Institute is a proposed organization. It has not been incorporated. ClearGlass Institute is not currently a registered charity with the Canada Revenue Agency. Donations are not eligible for official charitable tax receipts. Documents in this folder are drafts and templates, not legal advice. Signed originals belong in the minute book, not in this repository.

Working copy of what goes into the Articles of Incorporation under ONCA (Form 5270) [L-07]. Nothing here has been filed. Three items are open and must be closed before filing, because articles cost money and a members' vote to amend.

## Open before filing

| # | Item | Why it is open | Who closes it |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Name | Institute is a restricted word for an educational body [L-09]; ClearGlass may need consent from ClearGlass Inc. [L-10] | Owner, with the search house or filing agent |
| 2 | Purposes | Charitable wording while unregistered has a tax consequence [L-23] | Charity lawyer or CPA |
| 3 | Dissolution clause | ONCA limits who may receive remaining property, by type of corporation [L-19] | Charity lawyer |

## Fields

**1. Name of the corporation**

ClearGlass Institute

Fallbacks, in order, if the search or the registry refuses it:

1. A name without the restricted word, for example ClearGlass Public Interest Centre.
2. A number name, with the Institute's public name registered separately as a style name [L-11].

The first draft's fallback (ClearGlass Public Interest Institute) is not listed. It keeps both risky words.

**2. Registered office**

Burlington, Ontario. Enter the full street address on the form. Do not write the street address in this repository.

**3. Number of directors**

Minimum 3, maximum 9 [L-01].

**4. First directors**

Three individuals, each 18 or older, not bankrupt, not found incapable [L-02]. Each signs a consent to act before filing [L-03]; template in `governance/DIRECTOR_CONSENT_TEMPLATE.md`. Names and addresses go on the form and in the minute book only.

**5. Purposes**

1. To advance public education in cybersecurity, artificial intelligence literacy, digital investigations literacy, and information integrity.
2. To research, document, and publish non-commercial frameworks that help the public understand digital risk, system transparency, and defensive security practices.
3. To convene educational programs, workshops, and open resources for students, community organizations, and the general public in Canada.
4. To collaborate with educators, researchers, and civil-society groups on public-interest digital safety initiatives.
5. To do all things incidental and ancillary to the foregoing purposes, provided that the corporation shall not carry on any activity for the private profit of its members, directors, or related parties.

Note for counsel: purpose 4 (collaborate) and purpose 5 (incidental and ancillary) read as activities and powers, not purposes. If a charity application is planned, the CRA will test each purpose on its own. Ask whether 4 and 5 should move to a powers clause.

**6. Classes of members**

One class. Each member has one vote at every meeting of members.

**7. Special provisions**

- The corporation shall be carried on without the purpose of gain for its members, and any profits or other accretions to the corporation shall be used in promoting its purposes.
- Directors serve without remuneration for acting as directors. A director may be reimbursed for reasonable expenses incurred for the corporation.
- No part of the corporation's income is payable to, or otherwise available for the personal benefit of, any member or director.

**8. Distribution of property on dissolution**

Kit wording, not yet cleared:

> Upon dissolution, and after payment of all debts and liabilities, the remaining property of the corporation shall be distributed to one or more qualified donees within the meaning of the Income Tax Act (Canada) with purposes similar to those of the corporation, and not to its members.

Question for counsel: the Institute may be, at different times, an ordinary not-for-profit, a non-charitable public benefit corporation, and a charitable corporation. Does this one clause satisfy ONCA in each state [L-19] and the CRA's requirement for a registered charity? If not, what wording does?

**9. Incorporators**

The first directors sign as incorporators.

## Before pressing submit

- [ ] Items 1 to 3 in the open list are closed in writing
- [ ] NUANS report is Ontario-biased and less than 90 days old [L-08], or a number name is being used
- [ ] Three signed consents are in the minute book
- [ ] The fee on the payment screen matches what was expected [L-07]
- [ ] The person filing is a first director or is authorized by them in writing

Filing is a legal act. A person does it. An agent may prepare this sheet and nothing more.
