# ClearGlass Institute: legal source register

> **Status notice.** ClearGlass Institute is a proposed organization. It is not yet incorporated and is not a registered charity. Files in this folder are working drafts and templates, not legal advice. Signed originals belong in the minute book, not in this repository.

Every legal rule the kit relies on is listed here with its sources and how far it was verified. Documents cite rules by id, for example `[L-01]`.

Checked on the dates shown using web search extracts. The session that built this register could not open ontario.ca or canada.ca directly (network policy), so no claim is marked PRIMARY_TEXT_READ. Upgrade a claim only after reading the cited provision on the official page.
