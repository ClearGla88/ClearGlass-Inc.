# Owner's kit of 2026-11-14: what was taken and what was not

> **Status notice.** ClearGlass Institute is a proposed organization. It has not been incorporated. ClearGlass Institute is not currently a registered charity with the Canada Revenue Agency. Donations are not eligible for official charitable tax receipts. Documents in this folder are drafts and templates, not legal advice. Signed originals belong in the minute book, not in this repository.

On 2026-11-14 the owner supplied a complete thirteen-section founding kit. It covers the same ground as this package, and much of its wording already appears here. It was compared section by section with this package and with the source register. This page records the result, so that the same points are not reopened when the kit is supplied again.

The kit is not stored here. Its sources were checked: it cites the Act through a single e-Laws address (`laws/statute/10n15/v13`). e-Laws labels that address the historical version for December 2, 2021 to February 28, 2022. This package's register uses the consolidation from October 1, 2025.

## Not taken, and why

| The kit says | This package says | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| Remaining property on dissolution goes to qualified donees | For a charitable corporation the Act allows a narrower group; the articles sheet now uses it | [L-19] |
| Not-for-profit now; public benefit and charity rules apply "if it becomes" one | With educational purposes the Institute is a charitable corporation, and a public benefit corporation, from incorporation. Path A or B is decided before filing | [L-29] [L-30] |
| Directors "elected by members where required by ONCA"; the organizational meeting has no members' business | The first directors' term ends at the first members' meeting, which must elect directors. Step 9 of the plan does this | [L-31] |
| The 14-day plan starts by filing on day 1 | Written legal and tax advice comes first (stage 0). Fixing filed articles takes a members' special resolution | [L-42] |
| Day 8: apply for a CRA Business Number | The CRA says an Ontario incorporation is assigned one automatically. Step 12 confirms the number and applies only if none was issued | [L-26] |
| Directors may receive "compensation approved in accordance with applicable law" | Directors are not paid for acting as directors; charity law limits payments to directors of a charitable corporation | [L-30] |
| The board "may purchase" directors' and officers' insurance | Only as the Act and charity law permit, after legal review | [L-30] |
| Conflicted directors recuse "except as permitted by ONCA" | The remaining directors are deemed a quorum; if every director is conflicted, the members decide | [L-32] |
| Annual meeting "within the period required by ONCA"; initial return "as required" | Annual meeting within 18 then 15 months, and within six months of the year end; initial return within 60 days; notice of change within 15 days | [L-15] [L-16] [L-12] [L-14] |
| Payments "over $500" need two signatures; the expense table says "$500 or more" | One threshold: more than $500 | doc 6 |
| A "Share capital: none" field in the articles | No field needed; an ONCA corporation has no share capital | [L-43] |

Nothing in the kit changes `data/status.json`. No condition or milestone was recorded as met.
