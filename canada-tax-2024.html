Important Tax Notes — Nota Bene
Canadian law citations · CRA administrative positions · Filing obligations
1
OAS Clawback (Repayment)
ITA §180.2: Old Age Security repayment applies when net income (Line 23600) exceeds $90,997 in 2024. The clawback is 15¢ per dollar above this threshold, up to full repayment at approximately $148,535. Withheld through monthly OAS payments via T4A(OAS) Box 22. File Schedule 1 to declare. ITA §180.2(2)
2
Alternative Minimum Tax (AMT)
Budget 2024 — ITA §127.5: New AMT rate is 20.5% (up from 15%) effective 2024. Basic exemption raised to $173,205. Capital gains inclusion for AMT: 100%. Charitable donation deduction limited to 50% for AMT. If AMT exceeds regular tax, you pay AMT. Carry-forward of AMT credit available 7 years. ITA §127.52
3
Capital Gains — 2024 Changes
Budget 2024 — ITA §38: Individuals: first $250,000 net annual gains at 50% inclusion. Above $250,000: ⅔ (66.67%) inclusion. The $250,000 annual threshold resets each year — cannot be carried forward. Capital gains realized before June 25, 2024 use old 50% rate. Lifetime Capital Gains Exemption (LCGE) for QSBC shares: $1,016,602 in 2024. ITA §38(a)
4
New Brunswick — Provincial Notes
NB 2024 brackets: 9.4% on first $47,715; 14% on $47,715–$95,431; 16% on $95,431–$176,756; 19.5% above $176,756. BPA: $12,458. HST: 15% (federal 5% + provincial 10%). NB small business rate: 2.5% on active income ≤ $500K. NB political contribution credit up to $500. Volunteer firefighter/search & rescue: $3,000 credit available. NB Income Tax Act
5
RRSP / FHSA / TFSA
RRSP (ITA §146): 2024 limit $31,560 (18% of 2023 earned income). Contributions deductible; withdrawals taxable. FHSA (ITA §146.6): Annual limit $8,000, lifetime $40,000 — first-time buyers only, contributions deductible, qualifying withdrawals tax-free. Must close within 15 years. TFSA: 2024 contribution room +$7,000. Growth and withdrawals tax-free. Over-contributions penalized 1%/month.
6
Medical Expenses Threshold
ITA §118.2: You can claim eligible medical expenses exceeding the lesser of $2,635 or 3% of your net income (Line 23600). Eligible expenses include prescription drugs, dental, vision, mobility aids, attendant care. Supporting persons can claim unused amounts from a lower-income spouse. Medical Expense Supplement (refundable) up to $1,383 for low-income earners — Form T1, Line 45200. ITA §118.2(2)
7
Self-Employment Obligations
Self-employed individuals file by June 15, 2025 but any taxes owing are due April 30, 2025. File T2125 (business/professional), T2042 (farming), T2121 (fishing). Both employee and employer CPP contributions required (11.90% total in 2024) — employee portion deductible. Install tax (quarterly) required when balance owing >$3,000. HST/GST registration mandatory above $30,000 gross revenue. ITA §150(1)(d)
8
Quebec Residents — Special Rules
Quebec residents file a separate provincial return (MRQ TP-1) with Revenu Québec. Federal abatement of 16.5% of basic federal tax applies. QPIP (Quebec Parental Insurance Plan) replaces federal EI parental benefits — employee rate 0.494%, max insurable earnings $94,000, max premium $464.36. Federal EI parental benefit not available. QC basic personal amount: $17,183. QC solidarity tax credit available. ITA §120(2)

  1. Not Professional Advice: This tool provides estimates only. It does not constitute legal, accounting, or tax advice. Results are for informational purposes only and should not be relied upon for actual tax filing.
