The Invisible Command Centre

02
HEALTHCARE — SURGICAL THEATRE

The Sterile Intelligence Surface

At a Hamilton Health Sciences surgical theatre, Artemis VI replaces the scrub nurse's paper-based instrument checklist and the surgeon's ceiling-mounted monitor with a single intelligent glass panel above the operative field. The panel remains fully transparent — sterile field integrity is maintained — until the surgeon's Neural Interface layer registers a deliberate attention gesture: the glass resolves into a 72%-transparent overlay displaying real-time patient vitals, imaging reference, and instrument inventory drawn from the facility's EHR integration layer.

Because all computation occurs on-glass via the QPU node, no patient data traverses an external network during the procedure — a critical PIPEDA and PHIPA compliance requirement. The BCI layer, informed by Neuralink's VOICE trial architecture targeting 140 wpm decoded speech, allows the surgeon to dictate operative notes, flag anomalies, and request imaging rotations without breaking sterile contact. Post-procedure, the AI-GLASS digital twin energy management confirms the panel consumed 40% less HVAC-coupled energy than the legacy display array it replaced — directly measurable against the O-I Glass UK 240-ton CO₂ reduction benchmark.

03
CONSUMER — SMART HOME ENVIRONMENT

The Living Glass

A residential Artemis VI window installation in a Toronto condominium operates as the home's primary ambient intelligence surface. During daylight, SPD-SmartGlass tinting responds automatically to solar angle — blocking 95% of IR radiation, reducing air conditioning load by up to 40%, and eliminating glare without mechanized blinds. When the occupant wakes and directs conscious attention toward the window, the PDLC overlay layer activates at 30% opacity: weather, calendar, transit delays, and a personalized security brief from ClearGlass's open-data feeds surface as a heads-up display while the exterior view remains visible.

The Neural Interface peripheral — worn as a lightweight temple-contact strip — allows the occupant to dismiss notifications, adjust tint zones, and navigate the morning brief through deliberate attention and micro-intention signals, with no voice command and no touch input. All data processing occurs on-glass. No personal data leaves the premises. The Glass Futures digital twin model running in the building's facility management system monitors energy consumption across all Artemis VI panels in real time, providing the occupant and building manager with verified carbon offset data against Ontario's net-zero residential targets.
